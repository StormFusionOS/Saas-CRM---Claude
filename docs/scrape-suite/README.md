- **Business Owners** - Stay informed about your online presence

## Getting Started

### Accessing Scrape Suite

1. Log in to your CRM account
2. Navigate to **Scrape Suite** from the main menu
3. You'll see the Dashboard with an overview of all metrics

### First-Time Setup

**Step 1: Add Keywords** (5 minutes)
- Navigate to Keywords
- Click "Add Keyword"
- Enter keywords you want to track (e.g., "pressure washing near me")
- Set your target domain and page

**Step 2: Add Competitors** (5 minutes)
- Navigate to Competitors
- Click "Add Competitor"
- Enter competitor domains and assign priority levels

**Step 3: Configure Settings** (2 minutes)
- Navigate to Settings
- Enable automated scraping schedules
- Set refresh intervals for backlinks and citations

**Step 4: Trigger First Scrape** (1 minute)
- Navigate to Jobs & Logs
- Click "Run SERP Snapshot" to capture current rankings
- Monitor progress in real-time

That's it! The system will now automatically monitor your rankings, competitors, and online presence.

## Quick Start Tutorial

### Tutorial 1: Track Your First Keyword (10 minutes)

**Scenario:** You want to track "commercial cleaning services" for your business.

1. **Add the Keyword**
   - Go to Keywords → Add Keyword
   - Enter "commercial cleaning services"
   - Set target domain: `yourcompany.com`
   - Set target page: `/services/commercial-cleaning`
   - Intent: Commercial
   - Click Save

2. **Capture Initial Ranking**
   - Go to Jobs & Logs
   - Click "Run SERP Snapshot"
   - Wait 30-60 seconds for completion

3. **View Results**
   - Go to SERP Explorer
   - Select your keyword from dropdown
   - View your current ranking and competitors

4. **Set Up Tracking**
   - Go to Settings
   - Enable "Daily SERP Snapshots"
   - Click Save

**Result:** Your keyword will now be tracked daily, and you'll see ranking changes over time.

---

### Tutorial 2: Monitor a Competitor (15 minutes)

**Scenario:** You want to track what your competitor "CleanPro Services" is doing.

1. **Add Competitor**
   - Go to Competitors → Add Competitor
   - Domain: `cleanproservices.com`
   - Name: CleanPro Services
   - Category: Direct Competitor
   - Priority: High
   - Click Save

2. **Run Initial Crawl**
   - Go to Jobs & Logs
   - Click "Run Competitor Crawl"
   - Select CleanPro Services
   - Wait for completion

3. **View Competitor Pages**
   - Go to Competitors
   - Click on CleanPro Services
   - View all indexed pages
   - Note page types and content

4. **Enable Weekly Monitoring**
   - Go to Settings
   - Enable "Weekly Competitor Crawls"
   - Click Save

**Result:** You'll be notified weekly when CleanPro updates their site.

---

### Tutorial 3: Audit Your Backlinks (20 minutes)

**Scenario:** You want to understand who links to your website.

1. **Run Backlink Audit**
   - Go to Jobs & Logs
   - Click "Run Backlink Check"
   - Enter your domain
   - Wait for completion (may take 5-10 minutes)

2. **Review Backlinks**
   - Go to Backlinks & Citations
   - View "Backlinks" tab
   - Sort by domain authority
   - Filter for DoFollow links only

3. **Analyze Referring Domains**
   - Switch to "Referring Domains" tab
   - Identify high-authority domains
   - Note domains with multiple links

4. **Export for Analysis**
   - Click "Export Backlinks"
   - Open CSV in spreadsheet
   - Analyze link patterns

5. **Check Citations**
   - Switch to "Citations" tab
   - Review NAP consistency
   - Fix any mismatches

**Result:** You now understand your backlink profile and can prioritize outreach.

## Best Practices

### Keywords

✅ **DO:**
- Start with 10-20 high-priority keywords
- Include both branded and non-branded terms
- Track long-tail keywords (3+ words)
- Set specific target pages for each keyword
- Review rankings weekly

❌ **DON'T:**
- Track hundreds of irrelevant keywords
- Use generic single-word keywords only
- Ignore search intent
- Forget to update target pages

### Competitors

✅ **DO:**
- Prioritize 5-10 direct competitors
- Set appropriate priority levels
- Review changes monthly
- Categorize by market segment
- Monitor their new content

❌ **DON'T:**
- Track every competitor in your industry
- Set everything to "Critical" priority
- Ignore small competitors
- Forget to update competitor list

### Backlinks

✅ **DO:**
- Check backlinks monthly
- Focus on high-authority domains
- Monitor lost links
- Track anchor text diversity
- Disavow toxic links

❌ **DON'T:**
- Obsess over total link count
- Ignore link quality
- Buy backlinks
- Neglect internal linking

### Citations

✅ **DO:**
- Ensure NAP consistency everywhere
- Claim all major directories
- Update info when it changes
- Check quarterly
- Fix mismatches immediately

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use different phone numbers
- Abbreviate address differently
- Ignore citation platforms
- Wait to fix errors

### Audits

✅ **DO:**
- Run audits monthly
- Fix critical issues first
- Track resolution dates
- Re-audit after fixes
- Document recurring issues

❌ **DON'T:**
- Ignore warnings
- Fix everything at once
- Skip retesting
- Let issues accumulate

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I check rankings?**
A: Daily snapshots are automatic. Review manually weekly or when making SEO changes.

**Q: Why are some keywords not showing rankings?**
A: Keywords may not rank in top 100, or may be too new. Wait 24-48 hours after adding.

**Q: How do I know if a competitor changed their site?**
A: Check the "Changed" filter on Competitors page or review Jobs & Logs for change alerts.

**Q: What's the difference between DoFollow and NoFollow links?**
A: DoFollow links pass SEO value ("link juice"), NoFollow links don't affect rankings but still drive traffic.

**Q: How do I fix NAP inconsistencies?**
A: Go to the Citations tab, click the platform link, and update your listing directly.

**Q: Can I track competitors' rankings?**
A: Not directly. Add their target keywords to your Keywords list to see where they rank.

**Q: How long does a backlink check take?**
A: Typically 5-15 minutes depending on the number of backlinks.

**Q: What happens if I hit API rate limits?**
A: Enable "Proxy Pool" in Settings to rotate IPs and avoid rate limits.

**Q: Can I export all data?**
A: Yes! Each page has an Export button for CSV downloads.

**Q: How do I get alerts for ranking changes?**
A: (Feature coming soon) Enable email notifications in Settings.
